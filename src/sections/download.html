SpreadPaper is a native macOS app released under the MIT licence. Every version ships from GitHub as a DMG and a ZIP, with no account to make and nothing to pay for.

The first launch needs one extra step

SpreadPaper is not signed with a paid Apple Developer ID, so macOS will not open it on the first try. This is normal for open source apps, and there are two ways past it. On macOS 15 Sequoia and later, Control-clicking the app and choosing Open is no longer one of them.

Through System Settings

  1. Drag SpreadPaper to your Applications folder and open it once. macOS says it cannot verify the app is free of malware. Click Done.
  2. Open System Settings, go to Privacy and Security, and scroll to the bottom. A line there says SpreadPaper was blocked to protect your Mac.
  3. Click Open Anyway, then confirm with Open. macOS remembers the exception.

Or in Terminal, before you open it

Removing the quarantine flag skips the warning entirely. Drag SpreadPaper to your Applications folder, then run this once.

xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine /Applications/SpreadPaper.app

Either way, you only need to do this once. After that SpreadPaper opens like any other app.
