The gallery

Every wallpaper you make stays one click away

Saving writes a preset: the image, where you put it, the zoom, the flip, and the times if it runs on a schedule. The gallery lists them all with a thumbnail, and marks the one that is on your screens right now.

Narrow the list to one kind from the sidebar, or search it by name. Every card carries a menu that renames a preset, duplicates it, shows its image in Finder, or deletes it and leaves the image exactly where it was.
