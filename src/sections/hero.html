One photograph across every monitor

SpreadPaper puts one image across every display, so the picture carries on over the frames instead of starting again on each screen. Keep it still, pair a light one with a dark one, or put up to 16 images on a schedule that follows the day.

Download for macOS View the source on GitHub

Free and open source under the MIT licence. Requires macOS 15 Sequoia or later on an Apple Silicon Mac.
