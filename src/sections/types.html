Three kinds

One image, a pair, or a whole day

Every wallpaper SpreadPaper makes is one photograph laid across the displays you actually have. What changes between the three kinds is how many photographs you hand it, and what decides when they change.

  • One image

    Static

    You pick a photograph, drag it into place on a canvas that mirrors your display arrangement, and it goes up on every screen as one continuous picture. It stays there until you change it. This is the kind to reach for when you have a wide photograph you want to look at all day.

  • Two images

    Light & Dark

    Give SpreadPaper two photographs, one for light appearance and one for dark, and macOS picks between them. Switch your Mac to dark yourself, or set Appearance to Auto and let macOS switch it on its own schedule, and the wallpaper follows on every display at once.

  • Up to 16 images

    Dynamic

    Up to 16 photographs, each with a start time you set on the clock. SpreadPaper writes them into a HEIC dynamic desktop carrying the time metadata macOS reads, and every display gets the same schedule, so all your screens turn over at the same moment.

    1. 06:00 Sunrise on the ridge
    2. 12:00 Midday over the peak
    3. 18:00 Evening light on the range
    4. 23:00 The Milky Way above the snow
