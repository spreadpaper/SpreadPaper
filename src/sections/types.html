Three kinds

One image, a pair, or a whole day

Every wallpaper SpreadPaper makes is one photograph laid across the displays you actually have, whether that is three of them or one. What changes between the three kinds is how many photographs you hand it, and what decides when they change.

Two of the three ask for no second display at all. A Light and Dark pair and a day on a schedule are worth having on a single screen, and a laptop on its own is a perfectly ordinary way to run this.

Static, one image

One photograph, every display

Drag a wide photograph into place on a canvas that mirrors your real display arrangement, and it goes up as one continuous picture. It stays there until you change it.

  • Light The beach
  • Dark The palms

Light & Dark, two images

A pair that follows the appearance

Hand SpreadPaper two photographs, one for light appearance and one for dark. Switch your Mac yourself and the wallpaper follows on every display at once. Set Appearance to Auto and macOS makes the switch on the same schedule Night Shift runs on.

  1. 06:00 Sunrise
  2. 12:00 Midday
  3. 18:00 Evening light
  4. 23:00 The Milky Way

Dynamic, up to 16 images

A whole day on a schedule

Up to 16 photographs, each with a start time you set on the clock. SpreadPaper writes them into a HEIC dynamic desktop carrying the time metadata macOS reads, so every screen turns over at the same moment.
